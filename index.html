Gym Trivia

what muscle group does the bench press target?

The bench press primarily targets the pectoral muscles (chest), along with the anterior deltoids (shoulders) and triceps.

what does the term "PR" stand for?

PR stands for "Personal Record" .

What is the name of the largest muscle in the human body?

The largest muscle in the human body is the gluteus maximus, located in the buttocks.

What does the term "RIR" stand for?

RIR stands for "Reps in Reserve".

What is the recommended amount of rest time between sets for strength training?

The recommended rest time between sets for strength training is typically 2-5 minutes.

What does the term "DOMS" stand for?

DOMS stands for "Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness".

What nutrient is most important for muscle growth and why?

Protein is the most important nutrient for muscle growth, as it provides the building blocks (amino acids) necessary for repairing and building muscle tissue.

What is the strongest muscle in the human body based on its size relative to its force output?

The strongest muscle in the human body based on its size relative to its force output is the masseter muscle, which is responsible for chewing.

What muscle is responsible for extending your elbow?

The triceps brachii muscle is responsible for extending the elbow joint.
